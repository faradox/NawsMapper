# 0011: Cues

- **Status:** done (pending her first show with cues)
- **Started / finished:** 2026-11-09 / 2026-11-09
- **Driven by:** [pro-features.md](../pro-features.md) P1-4

## Goal

She builds a show as a list of looks and runs it with one button: GO (Space in Show
mode) moves to the next look with a crossfade, or the looks follow each other on their
own for an installation loop.

## Scope

In:
- `Project::cues`: each `Cue` has a name, fade time, optional auto-follow and the looks
  of all surfaces.
- Cues panel under the canvas: ● Record, ▶ GO (shows what's next), Stop, cue cards
  (LIVE / NEXT, "changed since played" dot), click = next, double-click = play now,
  context menu (Play Now, Update with Current Look, Move Earlier/Later, Delete),
  settings row (name, fade, auto-follow).
- Crossfades: numbers glide, the rest crossfades by drawing the surface twice.
- Show mode: GO button, cue strip, `Space` = GO, `Esc` = finish fade / stop follow.

Out (explicitly deferred):
- GO from MIDI/OSC (P2-3), timecode triggers.
- Cues that only change some surfaces (today a cue records all of them).
- Fade curves other than ease-in-out; per-property fade times.
- Cues changing geometry (corners, shapes, meshes) or sources' own settings.

## Design notes

- **A look** is a surface's serialized fields minus `NOT_IN_LOOKS` (id, name, locked,
  corners, mask, path, warp, cutout). Like multi-edit, this works on `serde_json`
  trees, so new `Surface` fields become part of cues without code. Geometry fields
  must be listed there.
- **GO edits the document.** Applying a cue is an ordinary change (one undo step), so
  what you see in Properties is always the target look, and saving keeps it. During the
  fade the renderer gets `cue::crossfade(from, document, ease(t))` instead
  (`CuePlayer::render_project`). A GO during a fade starts from what is showing.
- **What glides.** Only floating-point numbers interpolate (opacity, tint, feather,
  texture transform, border width and colors). Integers are ids (sources), so they
  can't; enums and booleans can't either. Speeds under `animation` and the border's
  `flow` would make time-based motion jump if interpolated (position = speed × time), so
  they are excluded too. Anything that can't glide is crossfaded by drawing the old look
  and, on top, the new look with opacity × t. Hiding fades the old look out instead.
  For additive blend modes this is not an exact crossfade, which is fine for a transition.
- **Clock.** Fades and auto-follow use the wall clock, not the transport, so they run
  while the animation is paused (Blackout does the same).
- **Recording** puts the new cue after the chosen or live cue and makes it live ("you
  are in the cue you just recorded"), so GO goes on to the next one.
- **Space.** Space stays play/pause in the editor (designer habit), and is GO in Show
  mode (QLab habit).

## Verification

Unit tests (14 new): a cue restores looks but not geometry or names; numbers glide; a
new source and blend mode fade in on top of the old look; hiding fades out and showing
fades in; animation speeds switch instead of gliding; integers never glide; unknown
surfaces keep their look; easing; looks skip geometry; GO steps through the list and a
chosen cue plays next; the document takes the target at once while the renderer fades;
GO during a fade continues from what is showing; follow plays the next cue once; zero
fade cuts and Stop ends a fade; recording places and names cues, Update keeps the name,
moving and deleting.

Xvfb: recorded two looks (Column visible / hidden); the card showed "LIVE ●" after the
change; double-clicking cue 1 faded the Column in (captured mid-fade); in Show mode
Space played cue 2 and the Column faded out on the preview and the projector; the key
hints list Space GO and Esc.
